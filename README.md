A make.fun entry for "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run". It writes a two-line explanation of a shadow to `/output/entry.txt`: the first line explains a shadow to a 10-year-old through the analogy of a dark twin that copies your shape but never your colours, and the second line says honestly what the analogy maps (the matching shape, the way it moves with you) and what it leaves out (a shadow is only blocked light, not a thing, and it vanishes the moment the light goes out).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
